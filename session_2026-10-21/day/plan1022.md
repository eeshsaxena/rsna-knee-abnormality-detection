  any component, so the stack identity rests on the registry sha guard and receipts (CLAIMED stack/build.md).

  all submissions or of the two selected ones is not known here (GUESS: either is possible); the medal outcome does not depend on it.
- **The outcome model is the pre-read prior.** The real class will be observed; within a class the table conditions on the winner
  only (and margin as info). P(ERROR) is GUESS.
