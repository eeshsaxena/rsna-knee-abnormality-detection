# Adversarial critique of BOX_PLAN_1015.md (workflow wf_2e51000b-4bc), saved by main 10-15 12:15 IST

## Verdict
GO on KC1, with the fixes below. Demote the S6 member to fallback only. Fix the trainer pre-registration break before installing the shim. Everything I checked on the box was read-only. KC1 is the right new family: the GPU is not what limits us, memory is fine, and no OAI data is involved. The plan has four real gaps. (1) The shim breaks the trainer md5 pin in PREREG_TEACHER. (2) The hour estimates run about 10-15 % optimistic against MEASURED T_a. (3) KC_READY has too many gates, which makes a slip past the 17:45 hold likely, and then T_a f4 (about 0 EV) takes the GPU for 3.5 h. (4) The laterality and localisation checks are too weak or come too late: the gold74 swap test runs only after about 4.4 GPU-h, and about 5 % of studies fall in a weak side zone. The S6 member costs a lot for little EV, and it takes the GPU during the 10-16 midday to 10-17 window that KC2 needs. Even with the corrected times KC1 lands well before the 10-20 freeze, around 10-16 midday or afternoon.

## Problems
- PRE-REGISTRATION BREAK (MEASURED). ~/r7t_labels/PREREG_TEACHER.md pins the trainer ~/fast_build/code2/fleet_train.py at md5 3e62434a. Line 50 says 'A code change after this file's sha is recorded needs an amendment', and line 234 says 'unchanged'. The M4 shim changes that md5. So T_a f4 and any E3 T_b s1049 member would launch on a trainer that was never pre-registered, and the runner's LAUNCH line would log the new md5. The plan does not cover this.
- HOUR ESTIMATES ARE OPTIMISTIC. MEASURED for T_a: 3.49 h per fold (f0 01:35 to 05:05, f1 05:07 to 08:36); 1,733 it/epoch at 0.30 s/it, i.e. bs 2 x 4 views x 16 train slices = 128 ConvNeXt-T 224 images per step; 9.0 GB on the GPU. KC per study is 48 ConvNeXt-S 256 images. Per image that is x2.54 the FLOPs (8.70/4.47 GMAC x (256/224)^2), and there are x0.75 as many images, so x1.91 per study (MODEL). That gives about 18 min train + ~2 min eval per epoch, so f0 takes about 4.3-4.6 h (plan 3.9) and each all-data member about 4.9-5.3 h (plan 4.3). KC1 totals ~14-15 h against the plan's 12.5 (MODEL). Members finish ~06:00-07:00 10-16 if f0 starts 15:47, or ~11:30-12:30 if KC_READY misses the hold (plan says 04:35 / 09:30). The 10-16 slot-2 KC1 entry is at risk, and the S6 morning GPU slot slips to midday.
- KC_READY IS OVER-GATED. It needs 8 conditions: a Kaggle kernel round trip, 6 new scripts, a 458-study parity run, the C4 smoke, a 31-study CPU dry-run of fleet_train.py, and more. All of it has to happen in ~3.5 h on 12 shared cores. Missing 17:45 lets T_a f4 (3.5 h, ~0 EV) take the GPU and pushes KC f0 to ~21:15. C3 (serve parity), C5 (dread labels) and C9 do not affect f0 training. Also, under OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 the C4 smoke will run fold-0 eval on test458 (~90 studies x 72 ConvNeXt-S 256 images, ~30-40 min, MODEL) unless --max_steps also caps eval. Regression (b) takes ~15-30 min (MODEL). Either can breach the 15:20 cut-off for fleet_train.py processes.
- THE SIDE RULE HAS A WEAK ZONE THAT THE U-RATE GATE DOES NOT MEASURE. On the 458 box studies, |mean_x| quantiles are 1 % 8.8 mm, 2 % 12.3, 5 % 28.0, 10 % 42.0 (MEASURED, kc_side_train.csv). So ~5 % fall under the 30 mm threshold and use the tag or weak rule (25/458 = 5.5 %, MEASURED). All 3/114 header-vs-report disagreements sit at 8.8-26.5 mm. Those studies get med/lat swapped at train and serve time with real probability, while the U rate is only 1/458.
- THE CANON GEOMETRY IS ASSUMED CONSTANT. The canon assumes sagittal normal x < 0 and coronal/axial row x > 0 in every series. That holds on 458/458 (MEASURED) but is unchecked on the other 3,949 train studies and on the hidden set. kc_side_kernel records sag_nx only for the Fat_Suppression series with the most files. The d96 reader fills slots with _pick_series_for_slot on Fluid_Sensitive in CSV order (MEASURED, reader source), so the kernel does not check the series that actually fill each slot. The coronal/axial row sign is not recorded at all.
- MISSING-VIEW PRESENCE. In the d96 mask (MEASURED, rsna_index.csv) the SagPD block is empty in 119/4,407 studies (2.7 %). SagFS, CorFS and Ax are empty in 0. For those 119 the sag_pd view is black, yet the vbias prior routes ACL to sag_pd. The plan never says that kc_common sets present=False from the d96 mask. The d96 corpus also has no TE/TR, so contrast handling (constant ctr or contrast_emb 0) has to match between train and serve.
- THE G1 SWAP TEST IS UNDERPOWERED AND LATE. It uses the gold74 4-label mean: 74 studies with few lateral positives, SE ~.01. If 20-30 % of studies had their canon flipped the test could still pass, and a correct canon can fail by noise. It fires only after f0 has spent ~4.4 GPU-h. There is no visual check before the GPU starts.
- LABEL CHOICE WORKS AGAINST DIVERSITY. T_b = .4 v4 + .3 fleet OOF + .3 R7 OOF (MEASURED receipt); Spearman T_b~R7 .856, T_b~fleet .904. The K14 fleet already read negative on FLIP (FF20, MEASURED). The T_b-student read (S) is still pending. Two of the three KC members are trained on T_b, so they mostly distil the base, while the public +.001 recipe used dread labels. The dread member also trains last, so it is the latest to arrive.
- ZERO-EV FILLERS OCCUPY ~15 GPU-H while KC2 cannot start before the KC1 read (10-16 evening). T_a f4: MEASURED rho(T_a, T_b) .971, mean adds +.0000/+.0003. L13 f3/f4: MEASURED null, never used in a kernel, and OAI-trained.
- THE S6 MEMBER HAS POOR EV PER COST. It needs a 265 GB fetch, unzip, a ~250 GB px cache on the same disk, a bf16 to fp16 patched external trainer, a PAUSE handoff, its own amendment, and a 2-model decode inside the tight S6F time budget. That buys +1 to +4e-4 (GUESS) at P ~.25 unconditional, and the precedent is negative (HFR7-RS .955 vs .956, MEASURED). It holds the GPU 12.5-17 h (MODEL) during the 10-16 midday to 10-17 window that KC2 would need. Separately, the --i-have-user-authorisation-to-finetune guard: if it asserts permission over nartaa's code or weights, Eesh's OK for training on the box is not that authorisation.
- GATE DEADLOCK. Only a written FAIL verdict #SKIPs the AD lines. If KC_READY is FAIL (f0 #SKIP), or f0 crashes after the 45-min OOM window without running its end hook, ~/kc/gate/kc_f0.verdict is never written. The two AD lines then hold 60 s at every later pick forever.
- THE G2 GAIN BAR (+.0024 on gold74, SE ~.01) FILTERS ON NOISE. It is ~0.25 SE, on a quantity MEASURED not to predict the LB: D gained +.0054 on gold, yet FF20 read negative. It may veto a real KC (P ~.2-.3, GUESS).
- CROP COVERAGE. The 110/120 mm sagittal and 90 mm coronal boxes are centred on the FOV, covering about +-55 mm. The suprapatellar pouch (Effusion) and peripheral marrow (Contusion, Fracture) can fall outside; only the 140 mm axial view covers them. KC at .2 on those labels may subtract. Nothing says yet whether KC applies to all 12 labels.
- NON-ISSUES, so they need no extra work. V100 memory: KC at bs 1 gc0 is ~7.5-8.5 GB (MODEL, activations x0.80 of T_a's MEASURED 9.0 GB at bs 2), so OOM risk is low; keep gc0. Hidden KC cost: one KC member ~0.27-0.31 s/study/T4 (MODEL), consistent with the MEASURED TTR224 member at 0.27-0.32 s (1.71 TFLOP, both TTA passes); 3 members fit FLIP-AO. Decode drift: box pydicom 3.0.2 vs kernel 3.0.1, and corpus byte parity is already MEASURED. Disk: 6.7 TB free (MEASURED). OAI: KC and S6 are RSNA-only; the only OAI item is L13.
