## 2. What we learned (the reasoning)

1. **5 folds + flip TTA made our fleet as strong as the parent:** solo .939 -> .943 (+.004).
2. **Equal strength means W .50 is optimal.** K5 W .25 .945 -> W .50 .946. The model says W .4-.6 is flat. **Weight
   tuning is closed.**
3. **The fleet is still parent-like** (fitted LB correlation ~.91-.95). That caps the blend lift at ~+.003. The next
   lift needs something strong AND less parent-like.
4. **v4 labels are a dead end.**
   - OOF is scored against v4 labels, so it flattered v4-trained models (looked only ~.007 behind).
   - On the LB they are ~.016 weaker: TTR v4 solo .923 vs mix50 .939.
   - Mixing 2 v4 folds into K5 lowered both reads by one unit (K6-i7 .945 / .942).
   - **Diversity must come from a different ARCHITECTURE on mix50 labels.**
5. **Our members are too similar to each other** (OOF Spearman .92-.94). More seeds of the same recipe add only
   ~+.0005-.001.
6. **Parent-likeness ranking** (OOF Spearman vs the parent's Raptor arms, 867 fold-0 studies):

   | model | Spearman | OOF |
   |---|---|---|
   | J1 TTR mix50 | .896 | .8836 |
   | F1 mix50 | .889 | .8811 |
   | gated F1 | .872 | .8734 |
   | TTR v4 | .850 | .8764 |
   | EV2S v4 | .835 | .8635 |

7. Display rounding (+-.0005) limits every single comparison to ~.001. Two reads moving together (as K6 did) are
   trustworthy.
8. **Realistic ceiling** with what we have: ~.946-.950 (silver). Gold (.958) is out of reach without a merge or a
   new strong family. **The team merge (Eesh posts before 10-15) remains the biggest lever.**
